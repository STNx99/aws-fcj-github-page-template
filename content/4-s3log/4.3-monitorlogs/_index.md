In this step, you will learn how to monitor the logs and events generated by your Lambda functions and EventBridge rules to ensure your deployment pipeline works correctly.

#### Monitor Lambda Logs in CloudWatch

1. Go to the [CloudWatch Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cloudwatch/home).
2. Click **Logs** in the sidebar.
3. Find the log groups for your Lambda functions (e.g., `/aws/lambda/upload-function` and `/aws/lambda/deploy-function`).

![CloudWatch Logs](/images/4.test/001-monitorlogs.png)

4. Click on the log group and review recent log streams to check execution details, errors, and messages.

![](/images/4.test/002-monitorlogs.png)

#### Verify EventBridge Events

1. Go to the [EventBridge Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/events/home).
2. Click **Event buses**, then select the custom event bus named **upload**.

![](/images/4.test/003-monitorlogs.png)

3. Navigate to the **Monitoring** tab.
4. Click **View metrics in CloudWatch** to see invocation metrics and event processing status.

![](/images/4.test/004-monitorlogs.png)
![EventBridge Metrics](/images/4.test/006-monitorlogs.png)

---

By regularly checking these logs and events, you can quickly identify and troubleshoot issues in your deployment workflow.
